## Intent

A fast, punchy 30-second short: **"The Circle of Humanity."** Population choropleth
shows the blinding band across south + east Asia; an amber ring drops over it; then
eight rapid-fire megacity dives (Tokyo, Delhi, Shanghai, Mumbai, Dhaka, Karachi,
Jakarta, Bangalore) land their populations one by one. The closer pulls back to the
whole bright band: more humans than every other continent combined.

## Assets

- assets/plate.mp4 — clean geolayer 1920x1080@30 globe plate, 30.8s, no text (to be copied in after render).
- assets/vo-1..10.wav — kokoro `af_heart` narration, one clip per beat (open, 8 cities, close).

## Customizations

- HyperFrames owns ALL typography (the plate is text-free): animated open stat, per-city
  population punch-ins, the closing "THIS IS WHERE THE WORLD LIVES" card.
- Amber heedline on near-black, echoing the engine's inferno ramp / night-lights basemap.
